## How It Works

When someone donates to a **Cause** on Giveth, they’re not just supporting a single project, they’re contributing to a pool that is actively managed by a Donation Agent.

### The Donation Agent

The Donation Agent is an AI-powered Agent that regularly pulls data from Giveth and external sources to assess projects within each Cause. It considers:

- **Project Information** from Giveth (title, description, update history)

- **Social media activity** such as posts on Twitter and Farcaster

- [**GIVpower Ranking**](/givpower)

Using the factors above, the Donation Agent prioritizes funding distribution to projects within the Cause according to: 

- How relevant their content is to the Cause’s goals

- How robust their impact evidence is 

- How active they are on socials 

- How often they leave updates on their Giveth project

- The quality of the content on their project and socials 

- How much GIVpower they have

### Fund Distribution

Each donation to a Cause is automatically bridged and swapped into **GIV on Polygon** via [Squid Router](https://app.squidrouter.com/), with minimal user friction. Donors can contribute from most EVM-compatible chains and with various tokens, but everything is ultimately converted into GIV.

Every 24 hours, the Donation Agent distributes **5% of the Cause pool’s current balance** among the selected projects. The allocation follows this breakdown:

- **92%** to projects, distributed based on scoring

- **5%** to Giveth, to support platform sustainability

- **3%** to the Cause creator, as an incentive to curate and promote high-quality Causes
