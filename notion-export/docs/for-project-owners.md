## **What This Means for Your Project**

Joining a Cause connects your work to a larger movement and opens doors to exciting new opportunities:

- **Be part of something bigger by joining a movement of aligned projects**

- **Get discovered by new donors looking to support broad impact**

- **Access exclusive opportunities, like **_**Cause-only QF rounds**_** with matching funds**

## **What You Can Do Right Now**

To make sure your project is eligible for inclusion in a Cause:

1. Ensure that your project is [**Verified and GIVbacks Eligible**](https://docs.giveth.io/projectverification).

2. Add a Polygon recipient address (required for distributions)

3. Leave project updates at least every 6 months

:::info

#### How to make sure your project get’s the most out of Causes

A few tips to make sure the donation agent can properly evaluate the value your project is creating

1. Add an update to your project monthly

2. Ensure your project updates and project description have high quality and detailed content that explain your mission and demonstrate the impact your project is creating

3. Add your project’s Twitter/X and Farcaster handle to its social media data (and post often).

:::
